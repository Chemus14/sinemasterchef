How it works

Your cake, from idea
to celebration

Every custom order follows a simple process — no stress, just sweetness.

01

Get in touch

Fill in the contact form or send us a WhatsApp with your idea, date and serving size. We respond within 24 hours.

02

We design together

We'll share a proposal with flavours, sketches and a quote. You approve or refine until it's exactly right.

03

We bake with care

Your cake is made fresh using the finest local ingredients — no artificial additives, ever.

04

You celebrate

Pick up in store or arrange delivery. All we ask is that you send us a photo of the happy faces!
